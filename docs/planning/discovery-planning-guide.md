# DISCOVER Planning Guide

> **Status:** Draft v0.1  
> **Purpose:** Teach practitioners—and AI assistants—*how to apply* DISCOVER, not just what DISCOVER contains.

---

# 1. Purpose

The DISCOVER Planning Guide helps a practitioner decide **how to approach a discovery engagement**.

It does not replace the DISCOVER methodology.

Instead, it recommends:

- which DISCOVER stages to emphasise;
- which POPIT-V perspectives to investigate;
- which discovery techniques are appropriate;
- which workshops to run;
- which artefacts to update;
- what "good enough" looks like before moving forward.

The planning guide exists because no two discovery engagements are identical.

---

# 2. First Principle

Always begin with the business objective.

Do **not** begin with technology, templates, workshops, process maps or AI opportunities.

Instead ask:

> **What understanding are we trying to gain?**

Only after that question is answered should the practitioner decide how to apply DISCOVER.

# 4. Planning Rule 001 — Understand a Business Domain

## Objective

Develop sufficient understanding of an unfamiliar business domain to support future discovery, data product design, analytics or AI initiatives.

Typical situations include:

- joining a new organisation;
- taking ownership of a new business area;
- merger or acquisition activity;
- supporting a transformation programme;
- preparing for domain modelling or ontology work.

## Recommended DISCOVER Stages

Priority:

- ✅ Define
- ✅ Investigate
- ✅ Study

Begin Capture as understanding develops.

Validate continuously.

Delay Evaluate and Roadmap until the business has been sufficiently understood.

## Recommended POPIT-V Focus

| Perspective | Priority | Why |
|-------------|----------|-----|
| People | High | Understand decision ownership and tacit knowledge. |
| Organisation | Very High | Understand capabilities, governance and ownership. |
| Process | Very High | Understand how work flows and where value is created. |
| Information | Very High | Understand terminology, decisions and business rules. |
| Technology | Medium | Investigate only after business context is understood unless technology is the explicit objective. |
| Value | Very High | Understand why the work matters and how success is measured. |

## Recommended Techniques

- Stakeholder Mapping
- Capability Mapping
- Structured Interviews
- Discovery Workshops
- Process Walkthroughs
- Business Glossary Development
- Decision Catalogue
- EventStorming (where complexity warrants)

## Suggested Workshop Sequence

### Workshop 1 — Business Context

Focus on Organisation and Value.

Outputs:

- Stakeholder Register
- Capability Map
- Evidence Register

### Workshop 2 — Business Processes

Focus on Process and People.

Outputs:

- Process Maps
- Pain Point Register
- Decision Catalogue

### Workshop 3 — Information and Decisions

Focus on Information and Process.

Outputs:

- Business Glossary
- Business Rules Catalogue
- Golden Thread

### Workshop 4 — Validation

Outputs:

- Evidence Register
- Open Questions
- Broken Threads
- Agreed next steps

# 6. Exit Criteria

Discovery is sufficiently complete when the team understands:

- why the domain exists;
- which capabilities it provides;
- who owns the work;
- how value is created;
- how work flows;
- which business decisions matter;
- which information supports those decisions;
- the most important business rules;
- the principal pain points;
- remaining assumptions and unknowns.

Completion is not determined by the number of completed templates.

Completion is determined by confidence.

---

# 7. Practitioner Guidance

Before every recommendation ask:

1. What uncertainty am I trying to reduce?
2. What evidence do I need?
3. Which POPIT-V perspective is most likely to reveal it?
4. Which technique provides that evidence most efficiently?
5. Which artefact should be updated?
6. How will I know I have learned enough?

Every discovery question should have a purpose.

If you cannot explain why you are asking a question, reconsider whether it should be asked.

---

# 8. Relationship to DISCOVER

The framework explains **what** DISCOVER is.

This guide explains **how to apply DISCOVER**.

The methodology remains authoritative.

The Planning Guide provides decision support for practitioners and AI assistants.
