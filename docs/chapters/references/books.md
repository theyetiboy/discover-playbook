# Books and Major Practitioner References

This list contains influential books and practitioner bodies that inform DISCOVER. DISCOVER combines ideas from several disciplines; inclusion does not imply endorsement of every method or that the source uses DISCOVER terminology.

## Business Analysis

### International Institute of Business Analysis (IIBA)

**A Guide to the Business Analysis Body of Knowledge (BABOK® Guide), Version 3.**

Relevant areas:

- elicitation and collaboration;
- strategy analysis;
- requirements life-cycle management;
- requirements analysis and design definition;
- solution evaluation.

Official source: https://www.iiba.org/career-resources/a-business-analysis-professionals-foundation-for-success/babok/

## Business Architecture

### Business Architecture Guild

**A Guide to the Business Architecture Body of Knowledge® (BIZBOK® Guide), v15.0** — current Guild resource referenced by this playbook as of August 2026.

Relevant areas:

- capabilities;
- value streams;
- information concepts;
- organisation;
- strategy mapping.

Official source: https://www.businessarchitectureguild.org/

## Data Management

### DAMA International

**DAMA-DMBOK® 2nd Edition Revised.**

The 2024 revision is a maintenance update to DMBOK2. The DMBOK 3.0 initiative is under development and should not be cited as an established replacement until formally published.

Official source: https://www.dama.org/cpages/body-of-knowledge

### Dehghani, Z. (2022)

**Data Mesh: Delivering Data-Driven Value at Scale.** O'Reilly Media.

Relevant areas:

- domain-oriented ownership;
- data as a product;
- self-serve data platform;
- federated computational governance.

DISCOVER uses these as optional product/operating-model ideas rather than prescribing a data-mesh architecture.

## Product Discovery and Product Management

### Cagan, M. (2018)

**INSPIRED: How to Create Tech Products Customers Love.** 2nd ed. Wiley.

Relevant to product discovery, value/risk testing and empowered product teams.

### Cagan, M. & Jones, C. (2024)

**TRANSFORMED: Moving to the Product Operating Model.** Wiley.

Useful for organisational/product operating-model discussion.

### Olsen, D. (2015)

**The Lean Product Playbook.** Wiley.

Relevant to problem/solution fit, value propositions and iterative discovery.

## Strategy

### Rumelt, R. (2011)

**Good Strategy/Bad Strategy: The Difference and Why It Matters.** Crown Business.

Useful for diagnosis, guiding policy and coherent action.

### Rumelt, R. (2022)

**The Crux: How Leaders Become Strategists.** Profile / PublicAffairs.

Useful for identifying the decisive challenge or constraint.

## How to Use This List

Use sources to strengthen practitioner judgement. Do not copy a framework mechanically into an engagement. The business outcome, evidence and level of complexity should determine which techniques are appropriate.
