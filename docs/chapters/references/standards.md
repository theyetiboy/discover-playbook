# Standards and Formal Specifications

> Verify versions before regulated or production implementation. This list records the standards used to ground the DISCOVER playbook as of August 2026.

## Business Process and Decision Modelling

### BPMN 2.0.2

**Object Management Group (OMG)**  
*Business Process Model and Notation (BPMN), Version 2.0.2.*

Formal OMG specification:
https://www.omg.org/spec/BPMN/2.0.2

Use for formal process representation where the engagement benefits from standard notation.

### DMN 1.5

**Object Management Group (OMG)**  
*Decision Model and Notation (DMN), Version 1.5.*

OMG specification catalogue:
https://www.omg.org/spec/DMN

Use for formal decision requirements, decision logic and business-rule modelling where appropriate.

## Enterprise Architecture

### TOGAF® Standard, 10th Edition

**The Open Group**

Official source:
https://www.opengroup.org/togaf

Relevant to enterprise architecture, business architecture, information systems and technology architecture.

## Data Quality and Metadata

### ISO 8000 Series

**Data quality.**

Catalogue:
https://www.iso.org/committee/54158/x/catalogue/

DISCOVER references quality concepts selectively and always relates quality to a use case or decision.

### ISO/IEC 11179 Series

**Metadata registries (MDR).**

Useful for governed metadata, concepts and definitions.

## Information Security

### ISO/IEC 27001:2022

**Information security, cybersecurity and privacy protection — Information security management systems — Requirements.**

Official ISO page:
https://www.iso.org/standard/27001

## AI Governance and Risk

### ISO/IEC 42001:2023

**Information technology — Artificial intelligence — Management system.**

Official ISO page:
https://www.iso.org/standard/81230.html

DISCOVER uses it as a reference point for organisational AI management rather than as a substitute for organisation-specific governance.

### ISO/IEC 23894:2023

**Information technology — Artificial intelligence — Guidance on risk management.**

Official ISO page:
https://www.iso.org/standard/77304.html

## Semantic Web / Knowledge Representation

### RDF 1.2

**World Wide Web Consortium (W3C)**

The RDF 1.2 family is under active W3C standardisation in 2026. DISCOVER references the relevant current W3C documents rather than treating a draft status as a final Recommendation.

Current family documents:
https://www.w3.org/TR/rdf12-concepts/

### OWL 2

**OWL 2 Web Ontology Language, Second Edition.**

https://www.w3.org/TR/owl2-overview/

Relevant to formal classes, properties, individuals and ontology semantics.

### SKOS

**SKOS Simple Knowledge Organization System Reference.**

https://www.w3.org/TR/skos-reference/

Useful for taxonomies, thesauri and controlled vocabularies.

### SHACL

**Shapes Constraint Language.**

W3C specifications:
https://www.w3.org/TR/shacl/

Useful for validating RDF graphs and semantic constraints.

### PROV-O

**The PROV Ontology.**

https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/

Useful for provenance representation.

### DCAT 3

**Data Catalog Vocabulary (DCAT) — Version 3.**

https://www.w3.org/TR/vocab-dcat-3/

Useful for interoperable data-catalogue descriptions.

## Emerging / Optional

OMG's **Data Product Ontology (DPROD)** has appeared in the OMG specification catalogue as an emerging/beta specification. DISCOVER does not currently make it a normative dependency. Practitioners may review it where formal data-product semantics are relevant.

## Standards Principle

A standard should be selected because it improves:

- shared understanding;
- interoperability;
- governance;
- assurance;
- traceability;
- implementation quality.

Do not introduce formalism where a simpler business model is sufficient.
