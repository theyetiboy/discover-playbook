# Chapter 1 – Introduction

> *"Data is only valuable when it helps people make better decisions."*

---

# 1.1 About this Playbook

Organisations often invest heavily in technology platforms, cloud infrastructure, data warehouses, dashboards and Artificial Intelligence before they fully understand the business problems they are trying to solve.

The result is often fragmented reporting, duplicated metrics, inconsistent business definitions, disconnected systems and low confidence in data.

The **DISCOVER™ Data Product Discovery Playbook** provides a structured methodology for discovering, understanding and modelling a business domain before designing data products, analytics solutions or AI capabilities.

Rather than beginning with technology, this methodology starts with the business itself—its people, objectives, processes, information and measures of success.

The output of every discovery engagement is not simply a list of requirements. It is a shared understanding of how the business operates, how information flows, where value is created and where data can be used to improve decision-making.

---

# 1.2 Purpose

The purpose of this playbook is to provide a repeatable methodology for:

- Understanding business domains
- Discovering business capabilities
- Mapping business processes
- Capturing business knowledge
- Building enterprise ontologies
- Identifying valuable data products
- Discovering AI opportunities
- Prioritising initiatives based on measurable business value

The methodology is designed to be reusable across any business function, including:

- Finance
- Marketing
- Sales
- Production
- Rights Management
- Legal
- Human Resources
- Technology
- Customer Operations

---

# 1.3 Who Should Use This Playbook

This playbook is intended for anyone responsible for discovering, designing or delivering business and data capabilities, including:

- Product Managers

# 1.4 Guiding Principles

## Principle 1 — Business Before Technology

Technology should enable the business—not define it.

Always begin by understanding the business problem before discussing systems, reports or architecture.

---

## Principle 2 — Decisions Drive Data

Businesses rarely need more data.

They need better decisions.

Every report, dashboard and metric should ultimately support one or more business decisions.

---

## Principle 3 — Model the Business, Not the Systems

Systems change.

Technology evolves.

Business concepts endure.

Customers, Contracts, Products, Revenue, Rights and Programmes will still exist long after today's applications have been replaced.

The business model should therefore become the foundation of every data product.

---

## Principle 4 — Capture Knowledge Once

Discovery should never be a one-time exercise.

Every workshop should contribute to an enterprise knowledge base that grows over time.

---

## Principle 5 — Create Shared Understanding

One of the greatest outcomes of discovery is creating a common business language.

Definitions should be validated, governed and reused consistently across the organisation.

Where different teams legitimately use different meanings, those differences should be made explicit rather than silently collapsed.

---

## Principle 6 — Every Recommendation Must Deliver Value

Every recommendation should answer four questions:

- What problem are we solving?
- Why does it matter?
- How will success be measured?
- Who benefits?

---

# 1.5 What is Discovery?

Discovery is the structured process of understanding a business domain before designing solutions.

Discovery seeks to answer questions such as:

- What does this business function do?
- Why does it exist?
- What value does it create?
- How does work flow through the organisation?
- Which information is required?
- Which systems support the work?
- Where are the pain points?
- What opportunities exist for automation, analytics or Artificial Intelligence?

Discovery is not requirements gathering.

It is the process of building a shared understanding of the business.

---

# 1.6 What is a Data Product?

A Data Product is more than a report or dashboard.

It is a reusable business asset that combines trusted data, business logic and user experience to solve a specific business problem.

A good data product:

- Has a clearly defined business owner
- Serves a specific audience
- Answers known business questions
- Uses trusted, governed data
- Delivers measurable business value
- Evolves over time

Examples include:

- Executive KPI Dashboard
- Revenue Forecasting Platform
- Rights Availability Explorer
- Customer 360
- Contract Risk Monitor
- AI Metadata Generator
- Programme Performance Explorer

# 1.8 The DISCOVER™ Framework

The DISCOVER Framework provides the structure for every engagement.

| Stage | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **D — Define** | Agree objectives, scope and success criteria |
| **I — Investigate** | Understand the business and operating model |
| **S — Study** | Analyse business processes |
| **C — Capture** | Capture business knowledge and information |
| **O — Organise** | Structure findings into reusable assets |
| **V — Validate** | Confirm findings with stakeholders |
| **E — Evaluate** | Prioritise opportunities |
| **R — Roadmap** | Translate validated findings into outcome-led direction |

The DISCOVER Framework provides the end-to-end methodology.

At the heart of DISCOVER sits the **POPIT-V Framework**, which provides the lens through which every business area is analysed.

---

# 1.9 The POPIT-V Framework

DISCOVER considers business areas through six complementary perspectives.

Across an engagement, practitioners should consider all relevant perspectives. Individual workshops should focus only on those needed to achieve the session objective.

| Perspective | Focus |
|------------|-------|
| **People** | Roles, responsibilities, ownership and decision making |
| **Organisation** | Strategy, governance, capabilities and objectives |
| **Process** | Activities, workflows and operational delivery |
| **Information** | Business concepts, data, reports and metrics |
| **Technology** | Systems, applications, integrations and platforms |
| **Value** | Outcomes, benefits and prioritisation |

Together these perspectives reduce blind spots and create a stronger foundation for appropriate business, data, analytics, AI or technology interventions.
