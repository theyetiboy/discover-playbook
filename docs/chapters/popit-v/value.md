# Value

> *"Discovery has no purpose unless it leads to measurable business value."*

---

# Purpose

The Value perspective focuses on understanding why business activities exist, what outcomes they are intended to achieve and how success is measured.

Rather than concentrating on outputs such as reports, systems or processes, this perspective seeks to understand the business outcomes those outputs are intended to deliver.

The objective is to ensure every recommendation made during discovery can be traced back to a clear and measurable business benefit.

---

# Why This Matters

Successful organisations invest in initiatives that create value.

However, value is often assumed rather than measured.

Without understanding value it becomes difficult to:

- Prioritise investments
- Justify business cases
- Measure success
- Demonstrate return on investment
- Align delivery with business strategy
- Secure executive sponsorship

Understanding value ensures that discovery remains focused on outcomes rather than activity.

---

# Discovery Objectives

The objectives of the Value perspective are to:

- Understand business outcomes
- Identify measures of success
- Understand strategic priorities
- Identify benefits
- Assess business impact
- Understand customer value
- Identify improvement opportunities
- Support prioritisation

---

# What Good Looks Like

At the end of discovery you should be able to answer:

- What value does this capability provide?
- Who benefits?
- How is success measured?
- What happens if nothing changes?
- Which improvements provide the greatest benefit?
- Which opportunities align with organisational strategy?
- How will value be measured after implementation?
- How does this initiative support long-term business objectives?

If these questions cannot be answered confidently, additional discovery is required.

# Generic Discovery Questions

## Business Value

- What value does this business function provide?
- How does it contribute to organisational success?
- What outcomes are most important?
- What would happen if this capability did not exist?

---

## Success Measures

- How do you measure success?
- Which KPIs matter most?
- Which measures are reported to leadership?
- Which targets are currently being missed?

---

## Benefits

- Which activities create the greatest value?
- Which improvements would have the biggest impact?
- Which opportunities would customers notice?
- Which initiatives would executives prioritise?

---

## Costs

- Which activities consume the most effort?
- Which processes are expensive?
- Where are resources under pressure?
- Which systems are costly to maintain?

---

## Risk

- What are the biggest operational risks?
- Which risks concern leadership?
- What keeps the business awake at night?
- Which activities create compliance concerns?

---

## Prioritisation

- If only one improvement could be delivered this year, what should it be?
- Which initiative delivers the quickest benefit?
- Which opportunity creates the greatest long-term value?
- Which initiatives depend upon others?

---

## Future Value

- What new capabilities are needed?
- How could better information improve decisions?
- Where could AI provide measurable value?
- Which investments would transform the business?

# Typical Pain Points

Common issues include:

- Undefined business value
- Poor KPI alignment
- Conflicting priorities
- Benefits not measured
- Weak business cases
- Difficult prioritisation
- Limited executive visibility
- Unclear return on investment
- Projects delivered without measurable outcomes
- Technology-led rather than business-led investment

# Finance Example

## Discovery Focus

The objective is to understand how Finance creates value through financial control, regulatory compliance and commercial insight.

### Example Questions

#### Business Value

- How does Finance contribute to organisational strategy?
- Which financial outcomes are most important?
- Which services create the greatest business value?

#### Success Measures

- Which financial KPIs are reported to executives?
- How is forecast accuracy measured?
- How is month-end performance measured?

#### Cost & Efficiency

- Which finance activities consume the most effort?
- Which manual activities create unnecessary cost?
- Where could automation reduce effort?

#### Future Value

- Which improvements would improve financial decision making?
- How could AI improve forecasting?
- Which data products would benefit Finance most?

### Typical Outputs

- Finance Benefits Register
- Finance KPI Catalogue
- Finance Value Assessment
- Finance Prioritisation Matrix
- Finance Business Case Opportunities

# Marketing Example

# Chapter Summary

The Value perspective ensures discovery remains focused on business outcomes rather than outputs.

By understanding what success looks like, how value is created and how benefits will be measured, organisations can prioritise investment, justify change and ensure that data products, analytics and AI initiatives deliver meaningful and measurable business outcomes.
