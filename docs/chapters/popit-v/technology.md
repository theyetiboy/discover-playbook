# Technology

> *"Technology should enable the business, not define it."*

---

# Purpose

The Technology perspective focuses on understanding the systems, applications, platforms and integrations that support business operations.

The objective is not to perform a technical architecture review, but to understand how technology enables business capabilities, supports business processes and manages business information.

Technology should always be considered in the context of the business it serves.

---

# Why This Matters

Technology is often the visible part of a business capability, but rarely the capability itself.

Poorly understood technology landscapes can result in:

- Duplicate systems
- Shadow IT
- Manual workarounds
- Poor integrations
- Data silos
- High maintenance costs
- Technical debt
- Low user adoption

Understanding technology enables organisations to improve operational efficiency while supporting future data products and AI capabilities.

---

# Discovery Objectives

The objectives of the Technology perspective are to:

- Understand the application landscape
- Identify business-critical systems
- Discover system ownership
- Understand integrations
- Identify technical dependencies
- Assess technical debt
- Understand reporting platforms
- Identify opportunities for simplification and modernisation

---

# What Good Looks Like

At the end of discovery you should be able to answer:

- Which systems support this business area?
- Which systems are considered the source of truth?
- How do systems exchange information?
- Which systems are business critical?
- Which technologies are creating problems?
- Where are manual workarounds used?
- Which systems duplicate functionality?
- Which technologies constrain future change?

If these questions cannot be answered confidently, additional discovery is required.

# Generic Discovery Questions

## Applications

- Which systems do you use daily?
- Which systems are business critical?
- Which systems perform similar functions?
- Which systems are nearing end-of-life?

---

## Integrations

- How do systems exchange data?
- Which integrations fail most often?
- Which interfaces require manual intervention?
- Where is information re-entered manually?

---

## Data Platforms

- Where is business data stored?
- Which platform is considered the source of truth?
- How is data shared across the organisation?
- Where are duplicate datasets maintained?

---

## Reporting

- Which reporting tools are used?
- Which dashboards are trusted?
- Which reports are manually produced?
- Which reports are duplicated?

---

## Security

- Who has access to business-critical systems?
- How are permissions managed?
- Which data is considered sensitive?
- Are there regulatory or compliance requirements?

---

## Technical Debt

- Which systems cause the most frustration?
- Which technologies are difficult to maintain?
- Which platforms require replacement?
- Which manual workarounds exist because of technology limitations?

---

## Future Technology

- Which capabilities are missing today?
- Which systems limit innovation?
- What technology investments are planned?
- How could AI improve existing platforms?

# Finance Example

## Discovery Focus

The objective is to understand how technology supports financial operations, regulatory reporting and decision making.

### Example Questions

#### Finance Systems

- Which ERP platform supports Finance?
- Which systems manage budgeting and forecasting?
- Which applications support Accounts Payable and Accounts Receivable?

#### Integrations

- How does financial data move between systems?
- Which integrations require manual intervention?
- How are journals transferred between systems?

#### Reporting

- Which platforms produce financial reporting?
- How is regulatory reporting generated?
- Which reports rely on spreadsheets?

#### Technical Challenges

- Which finance systems are approaching end-of-life?
- Which technologies create operational risk?
- Which manual workarounds exist because systems are not integrated?

### Typical Outputs

- Finance Application Catalogue
- Finance Integration Map
- Financial Reporting Platform Inventory
- Finance Technical Debt Register
- Finance Modernisation Opportunities

# Marketing Example

## Discovery Focus

The objective is to understand how technology supports campaign planning, customer engagement and marketing analytics.

### Example Questions

#### Marketing Platforms

- Which CRM platform is used?
- Which campaign management platforms are used?
- Which customer data platforms exist?

#### Integrations

- How are customer audiences shared?
- Which marketing systems integrate with Sales?
- How are campaign results imported into reporting platforms?

#### Analytics

- Which tools measure campaign performance?
- Which dashboards are used?
- Which reports require manual preparation?

#### Future Technology

- Which marketing technologies are planned?
- Which platforms could benefit from AI?
- Which integrations require improvement?

### Typical Outputs

- Marketing Technology Landscape
- Marketing Integration Map
- Campaign Platform Inventory
- Marketing Analytics Architecture
- Marketing Technology Roadmap

# Chapter Summary

The Technology perspective provides an understanding of the systems and platforms that enable business operations.

By understanding applications, integrations, reporting platforms and technical dependencies, organisations can identify opportunities to simplify technology landscapes, reduce technical debt and create a stronger foundation for data products, analytics and AI solutions.
