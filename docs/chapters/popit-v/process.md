# Process

> *"Processes describe how an organisation delivers value. Understanding them reveals where improvements, automation and innovation can have the greatest impact."*

---

# Purpose

The Process perspective focuses on understanding how work is performed across the organisation.

It seeks to identify the sequence of activities, business events, decisions, handovers and dependencies that transform inputs into valuable business outcomes.

Rather than documenting processes for their own sake, discovery aims to understand how work is completed, where inefficiencies exist and where opportunities for improvement can be realised.

---

# Why This Matters

Processes connect strategy to execution.

Poorly understood processes often result in:

- Manual work
- Duplicate effort
- Operational delays
- Inconsistent outcomes
- Poor customer experience
- Increased operational costs
- Reduced productivity

Understanding business processes provides the foundation for process improvement, automation, data products and AI initiatives.

---

# Discovery Objectives

The objectives of the Process perspective are to:

- Understand end-to-end business processes
- Identify process owners
- Discover key business events
- Understand business decisions
- Identify handovers and dependencies
- Discover pain points and bottlenecks
- Identify opportunities for automation
- Understand how value is delivered

---

# What Good Looks Like

At the end of discovery you should be able to answer:

- What triggers the process?
- What outcome is the process trying to achieve?
- Who performs each activity?
- What information is required?
- Which systems support the process?
- Where are decisions made?
- Where do delays occur?
- Where does work become manual?
- How is success measured?

If any of these questions cannot be answered confidently, additional discovery is required.

# Generic Discovery Questions

## Understanding the Process

- Can you walk me through the process from beginning to end?
- What triggers the process?
- What outcome should the process deliver?
- How often does it occur?

---

## Activities

- What happens first?
- What happens next?
- Which activities are manual?
- Which activities are automated?
- Which activities consume the most time?

---

## Decisions

- Where are decisions made?
- What information supports those decisions?
- Are decisions documented?
- Which decisions require approval?

---

## Exceptions

- What happens when something goes wrong?
- Which exceptions occur most frequently?
- How are issues resolved?
- Which situations require manual intervention?

---

## Information

- What information is required?
- What information is created?
- Which documents are produced?
- Which reports support the process?

---

## Technology

- Which systems support this process?
- Which systems create delays?
- Where are spreadsheets used?
- Which integrations are critical?

---

## Performance

- How long should the process take?
- Which steps create bottlenecks?
- Where do delays occur?
- Which KPIs measure success?

---

## Improvement

- Which activities add little value?
- Which activities could be automated?
- Which activities should be removed?
- If you could redesign the process, what would you change?

# Finance Example

## Discovery Focus

The objective is to understand how financial processes support accurate reporting, regulatory compliance and effective financial control.

### Example Questions

#### Month-End Close

- What triggers the month-end process?
- Which teams are involved?
- Which journals are posted manually?
- Where are reconciliations performed?
- Which approvals are required?
- Which activities regularly delay close?

#### Budgeting & Forecasting

- How are budgets prepared?
- How often are forecasts updated?
- Which assumptions are used?
- Which systems support forecasting?

#### Accounts Payable

- How are invoices received?
- How are invoices approved?
- Where do bottlenecks occur?
- Which activities are manual?

### Typical Outputs

- Finance Process Inventory
- Month-End Close Process Map
- Budgeting Workflow
- Financial Approval Matrix
- Finance Automation Opportunities

---

# Marketing Example

## Discovery Focus

The objective is to understand how marketing campaigns are planned, executed and evaluated.

### Example Questions

#### Campaign Planning

- How are campaigns initiated?
- Who approves campaign briefs?
- How are priorities agreed?

#### Campaign Delivery

- Which teams participate?
- Which external agencies are involved?
- Which activities are manual?
- Where do delays occur?

#### Performance Measurement

- How is campaign performance reviewed?
- Which reports are produced?
- Which insights drive future campaigns?

### Typical Outputs

- Campaign Lifecycle Map
- Marketing Workflow
- Campaign Approval Process
- Marketing Automation Opportunities
- Reporting Process Map

# Chapter Summary

The Process perspective provides a detailed understanding of how work flows through the organisation.

By documenting activities, decisions, handovers and dependencies, organisations gain visibility into how value is created, where inefficiencies exist and where improvements can be made. This understanding forms the basis for process optimisation, automation, data product design and AI-enabled transformation.
