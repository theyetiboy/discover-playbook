# Organisation

> *"Before understanding how a business operates, you must first understand why it exists."*

---

# Purpose

The Organisation perspective focuses on understanding the strategic context of the business.

It seeks to identify the organisation's purpose, objectives, business capabilities, governance structures and operating model. Understanding these elements ensures that discovery remains aligned with organisational priorities rather than isolated operational improvements.

---

# Why This Matters

Many transformation initiatives fail because they optimise individual processes without understanding the wider organisation.

Understanding the organisation helps to:

- Align initiatives with business strategy
- Understand business capabilities
- Identify organisational dependencies
- Clarify governance and ownership
- Reveal strategic priorities
- Identify investment drivers
- Understand decision-making structures
- Support enterprise-wide change

---

# Discovery Objectives

The objectives of the Organisation perspective are to:

- Understand the purpose of the business area
- Identify strategic objectives
- Discover business capabilities
- Understand the operating model
- Identify governance structures
- Understand organisational dependencies
- Identify key stakeholders and decision makers
- Understand strategic priorities

---

# What Good Looks Like

At the end of discovery you should be able to answer:

- Why does this business function exist?
- What value does it provide?
- Which capabilities does it deliver?
- What are its strategic objectives?
- How is success measured?
- How does it interact with other business areas?
- Who is accountable for outcomes?
- How does this function contribute to organisational strategy?

If these questions cannot be answered consistently, further discovery is required.

# Generic Discovery Questions

## Business Purpose

- Why does this business function exist?
- What value does it provide?
- Who are your customers?
- What are your primary responsibilities?

---

## Strategy

- What are your strategic objectives?
- What initiatives are currently underway?
- What challenges are preventing success?
- How do you measure progress?

---

## Business Capabilities

- What capabilities does your team provide?
- Which capabilities are business critical?
- Which capabilities require improvement?
- Which capabilities are planned for the future?

---

## Governance

- How are decisions made?
- Which committees or governance forums exist?
- Who approves major changes?
- How are priorities agreed?

---

## Collaboration

- Which teams do you work with most?
- Where do handovers occur?
- Which teams are dependent on your work?
- Where do communication challenges exist?

---

## Organisation

- Has the organisation recently changed?
- Are responsibilities clearly understood?
- Where does work overlap?
- Which areas lack ownership?

# Finance Example

## Discovery Focus

The objective is to understand how the Finance function supports organisational performance, regulatory compliance and commercial decision making.

### Example Questions

#### Business Purpose

- What services does Finance provide to the organisation?
- Who are Finance's primary customers?
- How does Finance contribute to strategic decision making?

#### Strategic Objectives

- What are Finance's priorities over the next 12–24 months?
- Which transformation programmes are underway?
- What regulatory changes are driving investment?

#### Capabilities

- Which finance capabilities are core to the business?
- Which capabilities are outsourced or shared?
- Which capabilities require modernisation?

#### Governance

- How are financial decisions approved?
- Which governance forums review financial performance?
- Who owns budgeting and forecasting?

### Typical Outputs

- Finance Capability Map
- Finance Operating Model
- Governance Structure
- Strategic Objectives Register
- Finance Transformation Opportunities

# Marketing Example

## Discovery Focus

The objective is to understand how Marketing supports customer growth, brand awareness and commercial performance.

### Example Questions

#### Business Purpose

- What role does Marketing play within the organisation?
- Who are Marketing's customers?
- How does Marketing contribute to revenue growth?

#### Strategic Objectives

- What are Marketing's primary objectives?
- Which campaigns support strategic priorities?
- How is success measured?

#### Capabilities

- Which marketing capabilities are most important?
- Which capabilities are delivered internally?
- Which rely on agencies or partners?

#### Governance

- How are campaign priorities agreed?
- Who approves marketing investment?
- How are budgets allocated?

### Typical Outputs

- Marketing Capability Map
- Campaign Operating Model
- Governance Overview
- Strategic Objectives Register
- Marketing Transformation Opportunities
