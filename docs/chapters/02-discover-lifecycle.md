# Chapter 2 – The DISCOVER Framework

> *"A structured methodology for understanding a business before designing solutions."*

---

# 2.1 Introduction

The DISCOVER Framework provides a repeatable, end-to-end methodology for understanding business domains, identifying opportunities and designing valuable Data, Analytics and AI products.

Unlike traditional requirements gathering, DISCOVER is designed to progressively build understanding.

Every stage builds upon the previous one.

By the end of the framework, discovery has evolved into a prioritised roadmap of business outcomes supported by trusted knowledge, rather than simply a collection of workshop notes.

---

# 2.2 The Framework

```text
              DISCOVER

        Define
            │
            ▼
      Investigate
            │
            ▼
         Study
            │
            ▼
        Capture
            │
            ▼
        Organise
            │
            ▼
        Validate
            │
            ▼
        Evaluate
            │
            ▼
        Roadmap
```

Each stage has:

- A clear purpose
- Defined activities
- Expected deliverables
- Exit criteria
- Recommended workshops
- Supporting templates

---

# 2.3 The Golden Rule

Never progress to the next stage until the current stage has been validated.

Discovery is iterative.

If new information emerges later, return to the appropriate stage and refine your understanding.

---

# 2.4 D — Define

## Purpose

Establish why the engagement exists.

Agree scope, objectives, stakeholders and success criteria before engaging with the business.

---

## Key Questions

- Why are we here?
- Who requested this engagement?
- What problem are we trying to solve?
- What does success look like?
- What is out of scope?
- What assumptions already exist?
- What constraints do we know about?

---

## Activities

- Review background documentation
- Identify stakeholders
- Define scope
- Establish objectives
- Agree engagement approach
- Create discovery plan

---

## Deliverables

- Discovery Brief
- Stakeholder Register
- Scope Definition
- Success Criteria

---

## Exit Criteria

✔ Scope agreed

✔ Executive sponsor identified

✔ Stakeholders confirmed

✔ Success criteria documented

# 2.13 Discovery Principles

Every engagement should follow these principles.

1. Understand before recommending.
2. Validate before documenting.
3. Model the business before modelling data.
4. Capture knowledge once.
5. Create reusable artefacts.
6. Focus relentlessly on business value.
