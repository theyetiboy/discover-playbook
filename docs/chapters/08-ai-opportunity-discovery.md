# Chapter 8 – AI Opportunity Discovery

> *"The best AI opportunities begin with business problems, not technology."*

---

# 8.1 Introduction

Artificial Intelligence (AI) has the potential to transform the way organisations create, manage and consume information.

However, not every business problem requires AI.

The objective of AI Opportunity Discovery is to identify where AI can create genuine business value by improving decision making, automating repetitive activities and augmenting human expertise.

AI should always be viewed as an enabler rather than the objective.

---

# 8.2 Objectives

The objectives of AI Opportunity Discovery are to:

- Identify suitable AI use cases
- Understand business problems that AI can address
- Assess organisational readiness
- Evaluate risks and constraints
- Prioritise AI initiatives
- Define measurable business outcomes
- Ensure responsible adoption of AI

---

# 8.3 AI First? No.

The first question should never be:

> "Where can we use AI?"

Instead ask:

- What business problem exists?
- What decision is difficult today?
- What work is repetitive?
- What information is difficult to find?
- Where do people spend significant amounts of time?

Only then should AI be considered as one possible solution.

---

# 8.4 Common AI Opportunity Categories

AI opportunities generally fall into several categories.

## Knowledge Discovery

Helping people find information faster.

Examples:

- Enterprise search
- Knowledge assistants
- Policy search
- Contract search

---

## Summarisation

Reducing the effort required to consume information.

Examples:

- Meeting summaries
- Report summaries
- Executive briefings
- Document summaries

---

## Classification

Automatically categorising information.

Examples:

- Content tagging
- Metadata generation
- Document classification
- Customer segmentation

---

## Prediction

Supporting future decision making.

Examples:

- Revenue forecasting
- Demand forecasting
- Audience prediction
- Risk prediction

---

## Recommendation

Providing suggested actions.

Examples:

- Content recommendations
- Product recommendations
- Suggested next actions
- Decision support

---

## Generation

Creating new content.

Examples:

- Draft reports
- Business documentation
- Metadata
- Marketing copy
- Knowledge articles

---

## Conversational AI

Natural language interaction with business information.

Examples:

- Chatbots
- Data assistants
- Conversational analytics
- Business copilots

---

# 8.5 Discovery Questions

## Understanding Work

- Which activities consume the most time?
- Which tasks are repetitive?
- Which activities require copying information?
- Which decisions require significant manual effort?

---

## Understanding Information

- Which documents are difficult to search?
- Which reports require manual interpretation?
- Which information is frequently requested?

---

## Understanding Decisions

- Which decisions rely heavily on experience?
- Which decisions could benefit from recommendations?
- Which decisions require combining information from multiple systems?

---

## Understanding Opportunity

- Where do delays occur?
- Which work provides little business value?
- Which activities are prone to human error?
- Which activities could be partially automated?

---

# 8.6 AI Suitability Assessment

Not every opportunity is suitable for AI.

Consider the following:

| Question | Consideration |
|----------|---------------|
| Is there a clear business problem? | Yes / No |
| Is sufficient data available? | Yes / No |
| Is the data trusted? | Yes / No |
| Can success be measured? | Yes / No |
| Is human oversight required? | Yes / No |
| Are there regulatory concerns? | Yes / No |

AI should only be considered where the expected value outweighs the associated complexity and risk.

---

# 8.7 Data Readiness

AI depends upon quality data.

Assess:

- Data quality
- Data completeness
- Data ownership
- Metadata
- Business glossary
- Enterprise ontology
- Historical data
- Security classification

Poor quality data will reduce the effectiveness of AI solutions.

---

# 8.8 Responsible AI

Every AI initiative should consider:

- Privacy
- Security
- Bias
- Fairness
- Transparency
- Explainability
- Human oversight
- Regulatory compliance

Business users should understand when AI is supporting decisions and when humans remain accountable.

---

# 8.9 Evaluating Business Value

Every AI opportunity should answer:

- What business problem does this solve?
- Who benefits?
- What measurable outcome is expected?
- How will success be measured?
- What happens if we do nothing?

Examples of measurable outcomes include:

- Reduced manual effort
- Faster decision making
- Improved forecast accuracy
- Increased customer satisfaction
- Reduced operational cost

---

---

## Proposed AI Capability

---

## Expected Benefits

---

## Users

---

## Data Requirements

---

## Risks

What are the key risks?

---

## Success Measures

---

# 8.12 Deliverables

AI Opportunity Discovery may produce an appropriate subset of the following artefacts, depending on the maturity of the evidence and the decision being supported:

- AI Opportunity Register
- AI Opportunity Canvas
- AI Prioritisation Matrix
- AI Risk Assessment
- Data Readiness Assessment
- Business Value Assessment
- Assumption & Open Question Log
- Validation Plan

These artefacts should test whether AI is an appropriate intervention. They should also record simpler non-AI alternatives where those could achieve the desired outcome with less complexity or risk.

# Chapter Summary

Artificial Intelligence should be viewed as an enabler of business outcomes rather than a technology objective.

Successful AI initiatives begin with clearly defined business problems, trusted data and measurable outcomes. By systematically identifying, evaluating and prioritising AI opportunities, organisations can focus investment where Artificial Intelligence provides genuine and sustainable business value.

The next chapter introduces Prioritisation, providing a structured approach for evaluating discovery findings and determining which opportunities should be pursued first.
