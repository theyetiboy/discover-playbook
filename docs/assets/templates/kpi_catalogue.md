- adoption measure;

## Decision Link

For each important KPI ask:

1. Who reviews it?
2. What decision follows?
3. What action changes when the KPI changes?
4. How quickly must the measure be available?
5. What explanation is required?
6. What happens if the KPI is wrong?

## Checklist

- [ ] KPI definition is business-readable.
- [ ] Formula and rules explicit.
- [ ] Grain/time period understood.
- [ ] Inclusion/exclusion rules captured.
- [ ] Owner identified.
- [ ] Data source/provenance captured.
- [ ] Decision/action link visible.
- [ ] Target/threshold distinguished from actual.
- [ ] Conflicting definitions preserved.
- [ ] KPI traces to business value.
