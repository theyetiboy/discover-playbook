## 4. AI Capability Hypothesis

What capability is actually required?

Examples:

- classification;
- prediction;
- recommendation;
- anomaly detection;
- extraction;
- summarisation;
- semantic search / retrieval;
- natural-language query;
- generation;
- agentic workflow assistance.

Avoid starting with a vendor/model name.

- unauthorised action;
- regulatory impact;
- automation bias;
- model/data drift;

## Governance References

Consider relevant organisational policy plus external guidance such as:

- ISO/IEC 42001:2023 — AI management systems;
- ISO/IEC 23894:2023 — AI risk management guidance;
- NIST AI RMF 1.0 and the NIST Generative AI Profile where applicable.

The canvas is not a substitute for formal legal, security, privacy or regulatory assessment.
