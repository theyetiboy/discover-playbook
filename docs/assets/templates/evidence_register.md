## Guidance

- A stakeholder statement can be evidence that the stakeholder holds a view, but not necessarily evidence that the view is factually correct.
- Do not overstate what a source proves.
- Record contradictory evidence as separate evidence items.
- Prefer source names and locations that another practitioner can find again.
