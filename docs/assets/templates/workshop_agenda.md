# Ground Rules

Agree the following at the start of the session.

- Respect all viewpoints.
- One conversation at a time.
- Focus on understanding before solutions.
- Challenge ideas, not people.
- Stay within scope.
- Use the Parking Lot for off-topic discussions.
- Encourage participation from everyone.

# Facilitator Notes

Before the workshop:

- Review existing documentation.
- Confirm attendees.
- Prepare supporting materials.
- Test meeting technology.
- Understand workshop objectives.

During the workshop:

- Encourage balanced participation.
- Ask open questions.
- Listen actively.
- Challenge assumptions respectfully.
- Keep discussions focused on business outcomes.
- Record decisions and actions.

After the workshop:

- Review notes.
- Update discovery artefacts.
- Circulate actions.
- Validate findings with stakeholders.
- Schedule follow-up sessions where required.
