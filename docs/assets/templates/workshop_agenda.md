# POPIT-V Prompt Bank

Use only where relevant.

## People

- Who performs the work?
- Who makes the decision?
- Who owns the outcome?
- Where is specialist knowledge concentrated?
- Who is affected when this goes wrong?

## Organisation

- Which business capability is responsible for this outcome?
- Who owns the capability?
- Which teams or partners are involved?
- Which governance or approval forums matter?

## Process

- What triggers the process?
- What actually happens?
- Where are the hand-offs?
- Where does work wait?
- Which exceptions matter?
- Where are reconciliations or rework required?

## Information

- What information is required to make the decision?
- What does this business term mean?
- Which definitions vary by team?
- Which information is trusted or disputed?
- Which business rules affect interpretation?

## Technology

Use after the business context is understood.

- Which systems enable or constrain the activity?
- Where is information captured?
- Which integrations or spreadsheets matter?
- Where does technology create manual work or risk?

## Value

- Why does this matter?
- What happens if nothing changes?
- Who benefits from improvement?
- Which measure would demonstrate improvement?
- What is the cost, risk or opportunity created by the current state?

# Workshop Success Criteria

The workshop will be considered successful if:

- [ ] The session objective was addressed
- [ ] Relevant stakeholders contributed
- [ ] Evidence and stakeholder statements were distinguished
- [ ] Material assumptions were captured
- [ ] Relevant Golden Thread links were established or marked unknown
- [ ] Important contradictions were made visible
- [ ] Follow-up validation is clear
- [ ] The next logical DISCOVER step is understood

Completion of every POPIT-V section is **not** a success criterion.

# Facilitator Notes

## Before

- Review the Discovery Brief.
- Confirm the specific session objective.
- Review available evidence.
- Select relevant POPIT-V lenses.
- Define the Golden Thread focus.
- Choose only the artefacts needed.
- Prepare primary questions and probes.
- Confirm attendees and roles.

## During

- Encourage balanced participation.
- Ask open and neutral questions.
- Listen for decisions, rules, definitions and exceptions.
- Separate evidence from assumption.
- Challenge solution-first framing constructively.
- Preserve conflicting viewpoints.
- Keep discussions connected to business outcomes.
- Use the Parking Lot to protect the objective.

## After

- Review notes against source evidence.
- Update canonical artefacts.
- Preserve existing traceability IDs.
- Record new assumptions and open questions.
- Identify Broken Thread gaps.
- Circulate findings for validation.
- Plan the next DISCOVER step.
