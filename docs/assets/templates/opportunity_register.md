## Guidance

- Phrase the opportunity around an outcome, need or capability improvement.
- Do not name a technology as the opportunity unless the technology itself is the validated business need.
- A candidate data product or AI capability may be one intervention option.
- Prioritisation should consider value, evidence, feasibility, readiness, risk and dependencies.
