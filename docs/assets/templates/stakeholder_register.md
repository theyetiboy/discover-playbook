## Stakeholder Categories to Consider

Use only those relevant to the scope:

- sponsor / executive owner;
- capability owner;
- process owner;
- operational user;
- business decision maker;
- approver / controller;
- subject-matter expert;
- information / KPI owner;
- data owner / steward;
- product owner;
- analyst / insight consumer;
- system / platform owner;
- risk / legal / privacy / security;
- finance / commercial;
- customer / partner;
- supplier / agency / research provider;
- regulator / auditor;
- governance forum;
- change / adoption community.

## Influence, Impact and Knowledge

Assess these dimensions separately.

### Influence
Can the stakeholder approve, block or materially redirect the work?

### Impact
How strongly is the stakeholder affected by the current problem or future change?

### Knowledge
How much relevant domain knowledge does the stakeholder hold?

A low-influence operational role may hold critical knowledge. Do not prioritise interviews solely by seniority.
