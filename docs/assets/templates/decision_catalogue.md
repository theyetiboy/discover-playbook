## Guidance

- Phrase the decision as a business choice, not as a report or system requirement.
- Do not invent a decision owner.
- If different stakeholders describe the decision differently, retain the conflict until validated.
- Link information and rules using their existing IDs where possible.
- A dashboard is not a decision.
