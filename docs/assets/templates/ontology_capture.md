# Example

## Concept

**Programme**

### Definition

A television programme commissioned, acquired or distributed by the organisation.

### Relationships

Programme **belongs to** Brand

Programme **contains** Episode

Programme **licensed through** Contract

Programme **broadcast on** Channel

Programme **generates** Revenue

Programme **measured by** Audience Rating

Programme **marketed through** Campaign

Programme **supported by** Rights Agreement

### Business Rules

- Every Programme has one unique Programme Identifier.
- A Programme may contain one or more Episodes.
- A Programme may be distributed across multiple Territories.
- A Programme may have multiple Licensing Agreements.
