## Definition Guidance

A good business definition should:

- use business language;
- distinguish the concept from related concepts;
- avoid circularity;
- avoid depending on one implementation;
- state important boundaries;
- be testable with examples.

Avoid:

> Revenue is the `revenue_amount` field in the Finance table.

Prefer a business definition whose precise wording can be validated by the domain owner.
