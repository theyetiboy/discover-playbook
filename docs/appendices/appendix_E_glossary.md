# Appendix E — DISCOVER Glossary

> This glossary defines terms **as used by the DISCOVER methodology**. Domain-specific business terms belong in the engagement's Business Glossary.

## A

### Action
A business response taken after a decision or insight. Golden Thread prefix: `ACT-`.

### Adaptive
Level 5 of the DISCOVER maturity model. The dimension is continuously improved using evidence and changing context.

### Assumption
A plausible statement that has not yet been sufficiently validated. Prefix: `ASM-`.

## B

### Bounded Context
A Domain-Driven Design concept describing a boundary within which a model and language are consistent.

### Broken Thread
A missing, unsupported or unclear relationship in the DISCOVER Golden Thread. Broken Threads should be surfaced rather than filled by assumption.

### Business Capability
An ability the organisation needs in order to achieve an outcome. Prefix: `CAP-`.

### Business Decision
A choice that drives action in the business. Prefix: `DEC-`.

### Business Glossary
A governed collection of business terms, definitions, context, ownership and semantic relationships.

### Business Information
The concepts, facts, measures, documents and knowledge required to operate the business and make decisions. Prefix: `INF-`.

### Business Rule
A statement that constrains, derives, classifies, approves or otherwise governs business behaviour. Prefix: `BR-`.

## C

### Capability Map
A model of business capabilities, independent from current processes, organisation structures and technologies.

### Capture
The fourth stage of DISCOVER: record evidence and findings in reusable structured artefacts.

### Competency Question
A business question used to define and test the useful scope of an ontology.

### Concept
An important type of thing in a business domain, such as Contract, Content, Customer or Territory.

### Confidence
An assessment of how strongly evidence supports a finding. DISCOVER commonly uses Low / Medium / High.

## D

### Data Asset
A physical dataset, table, file, stream, API resource or other managed data resource. Prefix: `DAT-`.

### Data Product
A deliberately owned and managed data capability serving identifiable consumers and decisions, with explicit quality/service/governance/value expectations. Prefix: `DP-`.

### Define
The first stage of DISCOVER: establish why discovery is taking place, scope, outcome and context.

### Developing
Level 2 of the maturity model.

### DISCOVER
The lifecycle: Define, Investigate, Study, Capture, Organise, Validate, Evaluate, Roadmap.

### Domain
A coherent area of business knowledge, rules and activity.

### Domain Event
A meaningful thing that has happened in the business, used extensively in EventStorming and domain modelling.

## E

### Evidence
Information explicitly supported by an observed or trusted source. Prefix: `EVD-`.

### Evaluate
The seventh stage of DISCOVER: assess root cause, impact, value, risk, feasibility, dependencies and opportunities.

### EventStorming
A collaborative discovery technique that explores a business domain through significant events, actors, commands, policies and hotspots.

## F

### Fragmented
Level 1 of the maturity model.

## G

### Golden Thread
DISCOVER's business traceability chain:

Strategy → Capability → Process → Decision → Information → Rules → Technology/Data → Data Products/Analytics → Insight/Action → Value.

## I

### Information Requirement
Information needed to support a business decision or action.

### Insight
An interpretation of information that helps a person understand a situation or decide what to do.

### Interpretation
An analytical conclusion drawn from evidence rather than a direct fact.

### Investigate
The second DISCOVER stage: explore the business domain broadly before converging.

## K

### KPI
A key performance indicator: a measure important enough to indicate performance against an outcome or objective. Not every metric is a KPI.

### Knowledge Graph
A graph of connected facts/instances, often using explicit semantic models or ontologies.

## M

### Managed
Level 4 of the maturity model.

### Maturity Profile
Separate maturity assessments across POPIT-V dimensions. DISCOVER does not normally average them into one score.

## O

### Ontology
An explicit representation of concepts, relationships, constraints and semantics in a domain.

### Open Question
Information required but not yet known or validated. Prefix: `OQ-`.

### Opportunity
A candidate intervention or improvement identified through discovery. Prefix: `OPP-`.

### Organise
The fifth stage of DISCOVER: connect and structure captured knowledge.

### Outcome
A change in business/customer condition or performance. Outcomes differ from outputs such as reports or software features.

## P

### Pain Point
An evidenced area of friction, delay, cost, risk or poor experience. Prefix: `PAIN-`.

### POPIT-V
The six DISCOVER analytical perspectives: People, Organisation, Process, Information, Technology and Value.

### Process
A flow of work from trigger to outcome. Prefix: `PRC-`.

### Provenance
Information explaining the origin and history of a fact, dataset or conclusion.

## R

### Recommendation
A proposed action based on discovery evidence and evaluation.

### Risk
An uncertain event or condition that could affect an outcome. Prefix: `RSK-`.

### Roadmap
The eighth DISCOVER stage: translate validated learning into an outcome-led sequence of change. It is not a sprint/release plan.

## S

### Semantic Model
A representation of business meaning, concepts and relationships used to create consistent interpretation.

### Source of Truth
A phrase that should be contextualised. A source may be authoritative for one concept, process or time but not universally authoritative.

### Stakeholder Statement
A statement made by a stakeholder that has not necessarily been independently validated.

### Strategy
The business intent and choices that frame capability priorities. Prefix: `STR-`.

### Study
The third DISCOVER stage: investigate priority areas in depth.

## T

### Taxonomy
A controlled classification or hierarchy of concepts.

### Technology
Systems/platforms enabling or constraining the business. Prefix: `SYS-`.

### Traceability
The ability to connect a finding, decision, artefact or recommendation back to its evidence and business purpose.

## U

### Ubiquitous Language
A Domain-Driven Design concept: shared domain language developed between domain experts and practitioners.

### UNKNOWN
The explicit marker used when an important fact or relationship has not been established. It is preferable to an invented answer.

## V

### Validate
The sixth stage of DISCOVER: test understanding with appropriate business owners and evidence.

### Value
The measurable or meaningful business/customer outcome created. Prefix: `VAL-`.

### Value Stream
An end-to-end flow of activity and information that produces value for a beneficiary.

## W

### Wardley Map
A strategic map that connects user need, value-chain dependencies and evolution/maturity of components.
