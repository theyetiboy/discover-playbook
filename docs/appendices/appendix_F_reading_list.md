# Appendix F — DISCOVER Reading & Reference Guide

> DISCOVER synthesises established practices rather than claiming that every idea is novel. This guide provides useful sources for practitioners who want deeper grounding.

Use the edition/current status recorded in `docs/chapters/references/` and verify standards before formal implementation.

---

# 1. Business Analysis

## BABOK® Guide

**International Institute of Business Analysis (IIBA)**  
*A Guide to the Business Analysis Body of Knowledge (BABOK® Guide), Version 3.*

Useful for:

- elicitation;
- requirements life cycle;
- strategy analysis;
- stakeholder engagement;
- solution evaluation.

DISCOVER's focus on evidence, decisions and validation is compatible with disciplined business-analysis practice, while DISCOVER adds its own lifecycle, POPIT-V and Golden Thread.

# 8. Ontology, Semantics and Knowledge Graphs

## Ontology Development 101

**Noy, N.F. & McGuinness, D.L. (2001)**

Practical introduction to concepts, classes, properties and iterative ontology design.

## W3C Semantic Web Standards

Useful standards include:

- RDF 1.2;
- RDFS;
- OWL 2;
- SKOS;
- SHACL;
- PROV-O;
- DCAT 3.

Use formal semantic technology only when it produces useful interoperability, governance or machine reasoning. A business ontology can begin as a validated conceptual model.

# 10. AI Governance and Risk

## ISO/IEC 42001:2023

Artificial intelligence management systems.

Useful for organisational governance of AI.

## ISO/IEC 23894:2023

Guidance on artificial intelligence risk management.

## NIST AI RMF 1.0

The NIST Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework uses the functions:

- Govern;
- Map;
- Measure;
- Manage.

## NIST Generative AI Profile

NIST AI 600-1 provides additional guidance for risks associated with generative AI.

Use organisational legal, privacy, security and risk policy in addition to external frameworks.

# 13. Suggested Reading Path

## New to DISCOVER

1. DISCOVER introduction and framework.
2. BABOK overview.
3. BIZBOK capability/value-stream material.
4. DMBOK data governance/metadata chapters.
5. Lean Product Playbook or Inspired.

## Business / Data Product Practitioner

1. Domain-Driven Design.
2. EventStorming.
3. Data Mesh.
4. Ontology Development 101.
5. relevant W3C semantic standards.

## AI / Semantic Product Practitioner

1. DISCOVER Information and Ontology chapters.
2. OWL / RDF / SKOS / SHACL / PROV.
3. NIST AI RMF.
4. ISO/IEC 42001.
5. AI evaluation guidance relevant to the chosen use case.

# Principle

References should improve practice, not turn DISCOVER into a literature review. Use the source that helps answer the discovery problem and preserve solution neutrality.
