# Finance Simulation — Discovery Brief

> Fictional worked example.

## Engagement Summary

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Engagement Name | Revenue Forecast & Recognition Discovery |
| Business Area | Finance |
| Sponsor | Finance Director |
| Discovery Lead | DISCOVER Practitioner |
| Review Date | End of initial discovery cycle |

## Why Are We Here?

Finance leadership reports that monthly revenue reporting is slow and forecast confidence reduces near month end. Analysts describe significant spreadsheet reconciliation and differing terminology between teams.

A dashboard and AI-assisted forecasting have both been suggested as possible solutions, but neither has been validated as the appropriate response.

## Initial Problem / Opportunity Statement

> Finance may lack a sufficiently consistent, timely and trusted view of forecast and recognised revenue to support month-end decisions efficiently.

**Status:** Assumption to investigate.

## Desired Business Outcome

Finance can make timely and confident revenue decisions using consistently defined information with reduced manual reconciliation.

## Discovery Objective

Understand how Finance makes monthly forecast and revenue recognition decisions, the process and rules involved, the information required, current sources of friction and the evidence needed to identify appropriate next steps.

## Key Discovery Questions

| ID | Question | Why It Matters | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| OQ-001 | What decisions are being delayed or weakened by current reporting? | Determines actual information need | Open |
| OQ-002 | What does "forecast revenue" mean across the teams involved? | Tests suspected definition conflict | Open |
| OQ-003 | Which rules determine whether revenue can be recognised in the period? | Establishes decision logic | Open |
| OQ-004 | Why is spreadsheet reconciliation required? | Tests root-cause hypotheses | Open |
| OQ-005 | How would Finance measure improvement? | Establishes Value | Open |

## Primary POPIT-V Lenses

- Process
- Information
- People
- Organisation
- Value

Technology will be explored only where it enables or constrains the discovered business process.

## Golden Thread Focus

Capability → Process → Decision → Information → Business Rules → Technology/Data → Action → Value

## Success Criteria

The first discovery cycle is successful when:

- the main Finance decisions are identified;
- material terminology conflicts are explicit;
- the recognition rule ownership is understood or recorded as unknown;
- the reconciliation pain point is evidenced beyond a single opinion;
- measurable value hypotheses are defined;
- the next validation activity is clear.

Completion of a dashboard specification is not a success criterion.
