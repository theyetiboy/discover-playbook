# Finance Simulation — Outcome-Led Roadmap

> Fictional worked example. This is strategic discovery direction, not a delivery schedule.

## Now

### Validate Revenue Definitions and Recognition Rules

**Related IDs:** OPP-001, INF-001, INF-002, BR-001, OQ-003

**Outcome**

Finance and commercial stakeholders have a validated shared understanding of key revenue concepts and authoritative recognition rules.

**Why now**

Conflicting terminology and an unvalidated rule affect the interpretation of later data and reporting work.

**Evidence**

EVD-004, EVD-005.

**Success measure**

Key definitions and rule ownership validated; conflicts explicitly resolved or governed.

---

### Evidence the Reconciliation Problem

**Related IDs:** OPP-002, PAIN-001, OQ-004, VAL-002

**Outcome**

The organisation understands exactly why manual reconciliation is required and has a reliable baseline for effort.

**Why now**

The current root cause is not established.

**Next discovery action**

Observe one close cycle or trace representative transactions across the current information flow.

**Success measure**

Root cause(s) evidenced and reconciliation baseline established.

---

### Establish Forecast Performance Baseline

**Related IDs:** OPP-003, ASM-002, OQ-005, VAL-001, VAL-003

**Outcome**

Current forecast performance and variance explanation time are measurable.

**Why now**

Without a baseline it is impossible to assess whether AI, process change or improved information actually adds value.

---

## Next

### Evaluate Intervention Options

Only after the Now horizon closes critical evidence gaps.

Potential options may include:

- process / ownership change;
- common definitions and governance;
- identifier mapping;
- trusted Finance information layer;
- analytical variance view;
- workflow automation.

No option is yet preferred.

Evaluate each against the decisions, evidence and value measures.

---

## Later

### Explore Predictive or Conversational AI

Only if validated evidence shows a use case where AI provides incremental value beyond simpler alternatives.

Potential questions:

- Does predictive modelling materially improve forecast performance above the existing process?
- Can AI explain forecast variance reliably enough for Finance use?
- What human oversight and governance would be required?

## Delivery Handoff Boundary

Detailed technical design, backlog creation, estimation and release planning begin only when an intervention has sufficient validated evidence and organisational approval.
