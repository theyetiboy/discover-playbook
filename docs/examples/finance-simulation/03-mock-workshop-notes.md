# Finance Simulation — Mock Workshop Notes

> **Synthetic evidence for methodology testing only.**

## Participants

Finance Director, FP&A Lead, Financial Controller, Revenue Accountant, Finance Analyst.

## Notes

1. The FP&A Lead says the monthly forecast is prepared from commercial submissions and adjusted for Finance assumptions.
2. The Revenue Accountant says "recognised revenue" means revenue that meets the accounting recognition rules for the period.
3. The Finance Analyst says one commercial team uses "revenue" to mean expected contracted value even where Finance would not yet recognise it.
4. The Financial Controller says final recognition approval sits with Financial Control.
5. The Revenue Accountant describes a rule: revenue cannot be recognised until the relevant contractual and delivery conditions are met. The exact policy wording was not available in the workshop.
6. The Analyst says three spreadsheets are manually reconciled because commercial forecasts, contract information and actual ledger postings use different identifiers.
7. The Analyst estimates the reconciliation takes "about two days" each month but no time-study evidence exists.
8. The FP&A Lead says late commercial changes cause forecast movements close to month end.
9. The Finance Director says the most important outcome is "confidence earlier in the month", not simply faster report production.
10. The Financial Controller says a dashboard displaying inconsistent definitions would make the problem more visible rather than solve it.
11. The FP&A Lead believes an AI model could improve forecasting, but no benchmark of current forecast error is available.
12. The group agrees that forecast accuracy, reconciliation effort and time taken to explain material variance are candidate success measures.
13. The group does not agree whether the main cause of reconciliation is poor data quality, inconsistent identifiers or different business definitions.
14. The policy owner for the recognition rule was not confirmed during the session.

## Closing Validation

Participants agreed that the notes accurately capture the main discussion, but the recognition policy, reconciliation time and forecast accuracy require further evidence.
