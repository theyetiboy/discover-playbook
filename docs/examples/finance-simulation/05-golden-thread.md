## Broken Threads

### BT-001 — Strategy

The business strategy or strategic objective connected to this Finance capability has not been established.

**Question:** Which strategic or financial objective makes earlier revenue confidence important?

### BT-002 — Recognition Rule Authority

`BR-001` was described by a stakeholder, but the authoritative policy and owner remain unknown.

**Question:** Which policy or accounting guidance defines the rule, and who owns its interpretation?

### BT-003 — Data / System Cause

The workshop identifies spreadsheet reconciliation but does not establish which system, identifier or data definition creates the mismatch.

**Question:** Trace one representative revenue item across commercial forecast, contract information and ledger posting. Where does semantic or identifier alignment break?

### BT-004 — Product / Analytics

No data product or analytics solution has been validated.

This is intentional.

A dashboard or AI capability should only enter the thread if it demonstrably supports `DEC-001` or `DEC-002` and contributes to `VAL-001`, `VAL-002` or `VAL-003`.

## Key Learning

The strongest current thread is:

**Finance capability**
→ **revenue decisions**
→ **information / definitions**
→ **actions**
→ **value**

The weakest part is currently:

**business rules**
→ **systems / data**
→ **candidate intervention**

That gap determines the next discovery activity.
