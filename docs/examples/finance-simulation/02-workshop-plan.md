# Finance Simulation — Workshop Plan

> Fictional worked example.

## Purpose

Understand how Finance currently forecasts and recognises monthly revenue, which decisions are made, which information and rules support those decisions, and where current ways of working create measurable friction or risk.

## Desired Business Outcome

Improve the timeliness, consistency and confidence of Finance revenue decision-making.

## DISCOVER Stages

- Investigate
- Study
- Capture
- Validate

## Primary POPIT-V Lenses

- Process
- Information
- People
- Organisation
- Value

## Golden Thread Focus

Process → Decision → Information → Business Rules → Data/Technology → Action → Value

## Participants

- Finance Director
- FP&A Lead
- Financial Controller
- Revenue Accountant
- Finance Analyst

## 90-Minute Agenda

| Time | Section | Objective | Evidence / Artefact |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | Context and outcome | Confirm why the work matters | Outcome / open questions |
| 10–25 | Process walkthrough | Understand current month-end flow | PRC candidates / pain points |
| 25–45 | Decisions | Identify actual business decisions | Decision Catalogue |
| 45–60 | Information and definitions | Understand required information and terminology | INF / glossary conflicts |
| 60–72 | Rules and exceptions | Understand recognition logic | Business Rules Catalogue |
| 72–82 | Pain, impact and workarounds | Test symptoms and consequences | Pain / evidence / value |
| 82–90 | Validation and next steps | Confirm understanding and gaps | Assumptions / open questions |

## Primary Questions

### Process

- What triggers the monthly forecasting and recognition cycle?
- Walk me through what actually happens from first forecast to close.
- Where are hand-offs, reconciliations or overrides required?
- Which exceptions consume the most effort?

### Decisions

- Which decisions do you make from this information?
- Who has authority to approve recognised revenue?
- Which decisions become harder or later because of the current process?
- What action follows each decision?

### Information

- What information do you need at the point of decision?
- What does "forecast revenue" mean here?
- Does another team use the same term differently?
- Which information is trusted, and which is challenged?

### Rules

- What must be true before revenue can be recognised?
- Where is that rule documented?
- Are there exceptions?
- Who has authority to interpret or override the rule?

### Value

- What happens when the process is late or wrong?
- How much analyst time is spent reconciling?
- Which measures would demonstrate improvement?
- Would a faster report alone solve the problem?
