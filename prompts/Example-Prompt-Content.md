# Content Discovery Focus

Use these prompts for content development, production, distribution, performance and portfolio decision-making.

---

# 1. Content Discovery Kick-off

> I am beginning discovery around content decision-making.
>
> Use DISCOVER to identify:
> - relevant business capabilities;
> - stakeholder groups;
> - major decisions;
> - information used;
> - business concepts;
> - processes;
> - measures;
> - technology/data dependencies;
> - value.
>
> Keep content performance measures separate from the decisions they are intended to support.

---

# 2. Content Performance Workshop

> Design a workshop to understand how the business evaluates content performance.
>
> Explore:
> - who evaluates performance;
> - when;
> - for what purpose;
> - what decisions follow;
> - which audiences/markets/platforms matter;
> - measures and definitions;
> - benchmarks;
> - qualitative signals;
> - commercial context;
> - limitations;
> - actions;
> - value.
>
> Do not assume one KPI represents overall content value.

---

# 3. Content Profile

> Stakeholders are interested in a reusable "content profile".
>
> Treat this as a candidate information/data product concept.
>
> Use DISCOVER to determine:
> - consumers;
> - decisions;
> - business concepts;
> - required attributes;
> - performance dimensions;
> - market context;
> - sales context;
> - rights context;
> - marketing/social context;
> - provenance;
> - freshness;
> - governance;
> - value.
>
> Identify what should be validated before designing the product.

---

# 4. Commissioning / Development Decision

> Help me investigate the decision to develop, commission, acquire or invest in a piece of content.
>
> Do not assume the precise decision model.
>
> Explore:
> - decision makers;
> - creative judgement;
> - audience insight;
> - market intelligence;
> - historical analogues;
> - costs;
> - expected value;
> - risk;
> - rights;
> - distribution opportunity;
> - social/cultural signals;
> - constraints;
> - actions.
>
> Separate evidence from hypotheses.

---

# 5. Global Content Performance

> Build a discovery plan for understanding content performance across multiple international markets.
>
> Focus first on the business questions and decisions.
>
> Then identify:
> - measurement concepts;
> - local market differences;
> - ratings/audience measures;
> - platform measures;
> - sales/licensing outcomes;
> - market intelligence;
> - social signals;
> - comparability issues;
> - metadata required;
> - governance;
> - value.
>
> Do not imply that measures from different markets are directly comparable unless the evidence establishes that.

---

# 6. Content AI Opportunity

> Explore AI opportunities in content insight and decision support.
>
> Start from user tasks and decisions, not AI capabilities.
>
> Evaluate candidate ideas such as:
> - content discovery/search;
> - summarisation;
> - similarity;
> - metadata enrichment;
> - insight synthesis;
> - conversational analytics;
> - opportunity identification.
>
> Include only ideas relevant to evidenced business needs.
