# Marketing Discovery Focus

Use these prompts for campaign planning, audience/customer understanding, channel strategy, performance and optimisation.

---

# 1. Marketing Discovery Kick-off

> Prepare a DISCOVER plan for Marketing.
>
> I want to understand:
> - strategic outcomes;
> - target audiences;
> - campaign planning;
> - decisions;
> - information;
> - channels;
> - measures;
> - operational processes;
> - current friction;
> - value.
>
> Recommend the POPIT-V lenses and workshop sequence.

---

# 2. Campaign Decision Workshop

> Build a workshop around how campaign decisions are made.
>
> Explore:
> - objectives;
> - audience;
> - proposition;
> - channel;
> - budget;
> - timing;
> - creative;
> - measurement;
> - optimisation;
> - decision owners;
> - required information;
> - actions;
> - value.
>
> Separate planning decisions from post-campaign measurement.

---

# 3. Marketing Performance

> Analyse how Marketing currently defines campaign success using the supplied evidence.
>
> Capture:
> - KPI;
> - definition;
> - owner;
> - calculation if established;
> - source;
> - frequency;
> - decision/action supported;
> - limitations;
> - conflicts with other definitions.
>
> Do not invent missing KPI definitions.

---

# 4. Marketing AI

> Explore whether AI could improve Marketing decision-making.
>
> Start with the business decision and user workflow.
>
> Assess:
> - information/data;
> - automation potential;
> - human judgement;
> - risk;
> - measurement;
> - value;
> - simpler alternatives.
