# Rights Discovery Focus

Use these prompts to explore the business management of content rights, availability, restrictions, obligations and exploitation.

Do not assume the current systems accurately represent the business model.

---

# 1. Understand the Rights Capability

> Help me prepare discovery for the Rights business capability.
>
> I need to understand:
> - what the capability exists to achieve;
> - core stakeholders;
> - major business processes;
> - important decisions;
> - business concepts;
> - rules/restrictions;
> - information sources;
> - downstream consumers;
> - value and risk.
>
> Use DISCOVER and identify the Golden Thread links we need to establish.

---

# 2. Rights Decision Workshop

> Design a workshop around the question:
>
> **"Can we exploit this content in this way, in this market, at this time?"**
>
> Treat that as a candidate business decision and investigate whether it is correctly framed.
>
> Explore:
> - decision owner;
> - content;
> - rights;
> - territory;
> - platform;
> - window;
> - exclusivity;
> - restrictions;
> - obligations;
> - approvals;
> - source information;
> - action/outcome;
> - commercial impact.
>
> Do not invent rights rules.

---

# 3. Rights Ontology Discovery

> From the supplied sources, identify Rights-related business concepts and relationships.
>
> Examples may include Content, Programme, Episode, Format, Rights, Licence, Agreement, Territory, Platform, Window, Restriction and Party, but include them only when supported.
>
> Separate:
> - explicit concepts;
> - explicit relationships;
> - inferred relationships;
> - unresolved terminology.

---

# 4. Rights Availability Pain Point

> Stakeholders say it is difficult to understand content availability.
>
> Treat this as an unvalidated problem statement.
>
> Use DISCOVER to identify:
> - what "availability" means;
> - who needs to know;
> - what decision they are making;
> - what rules determine availability;
> - what information is required;
> - where delays/errors enter;
> - consequences;
> - value of improvement.
>
> Generate discovery questions rather than a solution.

---

# 5. Rights Golden Thread

> Construct the Golden Thread for the Rights use case we are discussing.
>
> Start from the relevant business outcome and trace through:
> capability
> → process
> → decision
> → information
> → rules
> → technology/data
> → possible analytics/data product
> → action
> → value.
>
> Show unknown links clearly.
