# Global Sales Discovery Focus

Use these prompts for sales, licensing, customer, market and opportunity discovery.

Keep the business model separate from the CRM/system model.

---

# 1. Global Sales Kick-off

> I am preparing discovery with Global Sales.
>
> Help me investigate:
> - sales capabilities;
> - customer/market context;
> - content proposition;
> - opportunity lifecycle;
> - important commercial decisions;
> - information required;
> - measures of success;
> - friction;
> - value.
>
> Identify which DISCOVER stages and POPIT-V lenses should be prioritised.

---

# 2. Sales Decision Discovery

> Identify the decisions Global Sales needs to make across the sales lifecycle.
>
> Potential areas to explore include:
> - what content to pitch;
> - which customer to approach;
> - which territory/market to prioritise;
> - pricing;
> - negotiation;
> - licence structure;
> - pipeline confidence;
> - renewal/upsell.
>
> Do not assume these are the actual decisions; use them as hypotheses to test.
>
> Produce a discovery question set.

---

# 3. Customer 360 Challenge

> A stakeholder has requested a "customer 360".
>
> Challenge the phrase using DISCOVER.
>
> Establish:
> - who the consumer is;
> - which decisions it should improve;
> - what "customer" means;
> - required entities/concepts;
> - information required;
> - current sources;
> - current fragmentation;
> - actions;
> - success measures;
> - value.
>
> Do not jump directly to a dashboard or data model.

---

# 4. Content Sales Opportunity

> Help me investigate how Sales identifies the best content opportunity for a customer or market.
>
> Explore:
> - customer;
> - market;
> - territory;
> - content attributes;
> - previous sales/performance;
> - rights/availability;
> - commercial terms;
> - demand signals;
> - decision criteria;
> - information gaps;
> - actions;
> - measurable commercial value.
>
> Clearly label any candidate data/AI idea as a hypothesis.

---

# 5. Pipeline / Forecasting

> Build a workshop around sales pipeline visibility and forecasting.
>
> Focus on:
> - what leadership and sellers need to decide;
> - opportunity stages;
> - probability/confidence;
> - timing;
> - values;
> - ownership;
> - changes;
> - risks;
> - forecast adjustments;
> - actual outcomes;
> - definitions;
> - data quality;
> - reporting burden.
>
> Finish with evidence required before proposing a sales forecasting data product.

---

# 6. Conversational Analytics

> Explore conversational analytics for Global Sales without assuming it should be built.
>
> Identify:
> - high-value user questions;
> - decisions/actions those questions support;
> - business concepts needed;
> - semantic consistency required;
> - permissions/security;
> - source quality;
> - answer explainability;
> - risks;
> - measurable value.
>
> Compare the candidate idea with simpler alternatives.
